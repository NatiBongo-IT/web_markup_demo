Safety Tips

Important advice to help you stay safe and enjoy your trip in Ghana.

Go to Booking Contact us

Budget Planning

Set a daily budget, carry some cash in cedis, and keep a small reserve for emergencies.

Weather & Climate

Pack light clothes and sun protection. In the wet season, carry a rain jacket.

Visa Requirements

Check visa rules early and ensure your passport is valid for at least six months.

Local Customs

Be respectful, ask before taking photos, and follow local guidance for safe behavior.
